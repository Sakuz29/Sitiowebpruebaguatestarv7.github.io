Legacy giving

A legacy that opens doors for generations

Since 1952 we have supported children in the highlands. Your legacy can help us keep doing it.

  1. Home
  2. Get involved
  3. Legacy giving

What we build together doesn’t have to end

GuateStar’s story is also told in a book, IMPULSOS, about following God’s signs to leave a lasting legacy. A planned gift is your way of writing the next chapter.

A legacy means that a child who joins Club Extra today, and those who come after, have a complete path all the way to college.

Ways to leave a legacy

A gift in your will

You can leave GuateStar a specific amount, a percentage of your estate or what remains after providing for your loved ones. It’s the most common way to leave a legacy, and you can change it at any time.

Beneficiary of an account or policy

Name GuateStar as a beneficiary of a retirement account (such as an IRA or 401(k)) or a life insurance policy. You just fill out your financial institution’s beneficiary form, with no change to your will.

From your retirement account, during your lifetime

If you’re 70½ or older, U.S. law allows you to give directly from your IRA to a 501(c)(3) organization. Ask your advisor whether it’s a good option for you.
