Monthly giving

The Star Circle

Donors who walk with the children of Totonicapán every month, all year long.

  1. Home
  2. Get involved
  3. Monthly giving
Why monthly

A child doesn’t change their story in a month. Your support shouldn’t last just one, either.

We’ve learned that temporary aid doesn’t work. That’s why we build relationships that last for years with each child and family. Monthly donors do the same: they walk alongside.

  • Continuity: your gift sustains Club Extra week after week, not just a one-off event.
  • Planning: knowing what we can count on lets us open new centers and commit scholarships for the whole year.
  • Closeness: once a month we tell you what the children achieved, what’s still missing and who you helped.

Join the Star Circle

Every gift becomes education for a child in Totonicapán.

Give $30 monthly

Secure payment on coloradogives.org · in US dollars · emailed receipt

You’ll confirm the amount on Colorado Gives, which opens in a new tab. Impact examples are estimates based on the average cost of $30 a month per child in Club Extra.

What your monthly gift makes possible

$30a month: one child gets a full month of Club Extra, with academic support, mentorship and materials
$90a month: three children receive Club Extra every month, with academic support and mentorship
$150a month: five children have their Club Extra spot secured all year

Estimates based on the average cost of $30 a month per child in Club Extra.

How it works

  1. Choose your amount

    From $1 a day. You complete your gift on Colorado Gives with the monthly option turned on.

  2. Get your receipt

    Every charge comes with an emailed receipt, ready for your U.S. tax return.

  3. Follow their progress

    One letter a month with news from the children. And if you ever need to change something, just write to us.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Star Circle?

It’s the group of people who give to GuateStar every month. There’s no minimum amount: what matters is consistency.

Can I change the amount or cancel?

Yes, whenever you want. Write to us and the change takes effect before the next charge.

Do I get a receipt?

Yes. Colorado Gives emails you a receipt that is valid for U.S. tax purposes.

Your consistency is their opportunity

See the impact of your support, give today or join us as a volunteer. Every decision counts.
