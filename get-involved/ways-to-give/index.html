Colorado Gives Day · December 8 — Online gifts to GuateStar on coloradogives.org since November 1 count toward Colorado’s biggest day of giving.

Give on Colorado Gives

Every gift, in any size or form, counts

Your gift goes to the educational programs: scholarships, technology, mentorship and support for families in eight communities in Totonicapán.

Quick and secure

Give online

By card in US dollars through Colorado Gives, in a couple of minutes. Your receipt arrives by email.
