Your gift becomes education

Every gift goes to the educational programs: scholarships, technology, mentorship and support for families. See all ways to give.

Star Circle: give monthly

A child doesn’t change their story in a month. Your monthly gift walks with them through the whole school year. $30 a month gives one child a full month of Club Extra.
