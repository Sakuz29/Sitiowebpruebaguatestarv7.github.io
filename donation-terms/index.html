Transparency & legal

Donation terms

  1. Home
  2. Donation terms

Nature of the gift

Donations are voluntary, final and non-refundable. Once completed, they become part of GuateStar’s assets and are allocated to its educational programs.

Transaction errors

If you are charged twice or for an amount different from the one you authorized, write to us within 30 days and we will correct it. You will receive a receipt by email that is valid for U.S. tax purposes.

Tax deductibility

GuateStar is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization registered in the United States, so gifts from U.S. taxpayers are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. If you give from another country, deductibility depends on your local legislation. GuateStar does not provide tax advice: consult a professional before claiming your donation.

Currency and charges

Online amounts are shown in US dollars (USD), the currency Colorado Gives Foundation charges in. If you give from outside the United States, your bank or card may apply currency conversion and fees outside GuateStar’s control. From Guatemala you can transfer in quetzales to the account shown on the donation page.

Recurring gifts

You may change or cancel your monthly donation at any time by writing to us; cancellation takes effect before the next charge.

Use of funds

Gifts are allocated to the area of greatest need within the programs. The impact examples shown on this site are indicative and do not constitute a commitment to individual allocation.

Payment security

Payment is processed by a gateway certified under the PCI DSS standard over an encrypted connection. GuateStar does not store card details.

Scope

We accept donations from any country. GuateStar is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization incorporated in the United States, and these terms are governed by U.S. law; programs are carried out in Guatemala under local regulations. Applicable rules may differ in your jurisdiction.

Last updated: August 2026.

A US-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit breaking the cycle of poverty through quality education, holistic support and leadership development in the rural communities of Totonicapán, Guatemala.
