Questions about your gift

Is my donation tax-deductible?

GuateStar is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit registered in the United States, so gifts from U.S. taxpayers are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. If you give from another country, it depends on your local laws. You’ll receive a receipt by email. GuateStar does not provide tax advice.

Can I change or cancel my monthly gift?

Yes, at any time. Write to us through the contact form and the cancellation takes effect before the next charge.

Why do donations go through Colorado Gives?

Colorado Gives Foundation is the platform GuateStar uses to receive online donations from the United States. Payment is processed securely in US dollars, and you receive the receipt by email.

Can I give from Guatemala?

Yes, by bank transfer in quetzales to GuateStar’s account at Banco Industrial. If you need an invoice, leave your NIT in the transfer notice form.
