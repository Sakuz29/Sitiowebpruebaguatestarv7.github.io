What we do

A complete pathway, from Club Extra to college

In Guatemala, the challenge isn’t getting children into the classroom but keeping them there. So we walk with every child, and their family, through each stage: learning in community, having tools, receiving a scholarship, preparing for college and giving back.

The challenge

Only 11 of every 100 children who start elementary school reach college

Talent in Guatemala is unlimited. Opportunity is not. Informal work, migration, early pregnancy and being over-age for grade push thousands of children out of school, and Totonicapán is among the departments with the lowest college enrollment.

We refuse to accept this reality. In 2026, all 10 scholarship students who finished 9th grade moved on to high school, and 6 of 7 high school graduates went on to college.

Roots

The journey begins

We began supporting children in the Guatemalan highlands. Since then, one conviction has guided us: education breaks the cycle of poverty.

Decadesin the highlands
10 years ago

Club Extra is born

In Chuixacol, Momostenango, a village of just 18 families, seven children formed the first Club Extra. It all started with a program that decided not to let go of anyone.

7children in the first group
2023

Four communities

The program was supporting an average of 155 children and 98 families in four communities in Totonicapán.

98families supported
2024

Internet reaches the village

We installed our first internet antenna. Five communities and 220 children learning with technology right where they live.

The first graduates

We recorded the program’s first high school graduate and celebrated three college graduates.
