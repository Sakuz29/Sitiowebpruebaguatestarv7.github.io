Scholarships

A scholarship to stay in school, from preschool to college.

  1. Home
  2. The Pathway
  3. Scholarships

No child should stop studying for lack of money

Scholarships let girls, boys and young people keep studying from preschool all the way to college or a technical program.

From preschool through middle school, students attend Colegio Evangélico Metodista Juan Wesley and Colegio Evangélico Metodista Esmirna. After 9th grade, scholarships support high school, INTECAP and college.

57 of the 64 school scholarship students also attend Club Extra, and many serve as volunteers.

How a scholarship is awarded

Scholarships are awarded through responsible and transparent selection processes, to support families with real needs and a strong commitment to their children’s education. In return we ask for three commitments:

  • Grades: working hard to keep good grades.
  • Responsibility: following through and building good values.
  • Service: many scholarship students also volunteer.
