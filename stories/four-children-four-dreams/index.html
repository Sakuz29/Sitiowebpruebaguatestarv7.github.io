Four children, four dreams and a father’s sacrifice

A story of perseverance, integrity and opportunities that transform generations.

📍 TotonicapánThe Pathway: Scholarship → Stage 62026 annual report
  1. Home
  2. Stories
  3. Four children, four dreams and a father’s sacrifice

Every day, among fabrics, needles and sewing machines, Don Eric works as a tailor with a purpose that goes far beyond making a living: giving his four children the educational opportunities that will let them build a better future.

Despite the financial limitations and challenges his family faces, he has never stopped believing in the power of education. His effort, combined with his children’s perseverance and GuateStar’s support, has borne extraordinary fruit.

The story began in 2015, when his eldest daughter, Yamilet, received a GuateStar scholarship to complete the three years of middle school. That opportunity was the start of a path that would shape the future of the entire family.

Four children, four dreams

Yamilet

Holds a degree in Psychology. Works at a well-known educational institution in Quetzaltenango.

José Adrián

Is in his second year of Systems Engineering at Universidad de San Carlos de Guatemala.

The third daughter

Is preparing to become a preschool teacher.

The youngest

Excels in her studies and is getting ready to start 9th grade.

All four siblings have been scholarship recipients and Club Extra participants. Besides working hard to keep their grades up, show responsibility and build good values, they have also served as volunteers.

“When a scholarship reaches the right hands, its impact can extend far beyond a single student: it can transform the opportunities of an entire family.”GuateStar

Don Eric keeps working as a tailor, driven by the wish to see his children reach their goals. And they, in turn, share a dream: to become professionals and one day repay their father for all the effort and sacrifice he has made for them.

Behind these four young people’s achievements is a father who never stopped working, four children who chose to persevere, and donors who believed in their potential.
