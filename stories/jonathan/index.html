Jonathan: an opportunity that transforms lives

A story of hope from the mountains of Guatemala.

📍 Salitre de Conejo, MomostenangoThe Pathway: Stage 1 → Stage 22026 annual report
  1. Home
  2. Stories
  3. Jonathan

In the mountains of Momostenango, in a small community called Paraje Salitre de Conejo, lives Jonathan, a 15-year-old whose story shows how one educational opportunity can transform a life and begin to impact a whole community.

In 2020, when the pandemic closed schools, Jonathan was in 4th grade. GuateStar gave him a computer and access to MAYALINK, a local server that shared videos by Guatemalan teachers in the core subjects from 1st to 6th grade over a local wifi network, with no internet needed.

During a home visit, his mother, Doña María, shared with gratitude how her son was learning math and language in a more creative way. But Jonathan had a surprise: he had finished the 4th-grade videos and, on his own initiative, kept going with the 5th- and 6th-grade ones. That is how, without being able to go to school, he learned math during the pandemic.

Today, thanks to donations and GuateStar’s growth, Club Extra children learn with an internet connection in their communities, which makes the process even simpler.

“Jonathan wasn’t just learning: he had discovered the joy of learning on his own.”GuateStar team

His greatest achievement isn’t his medals

Later, during a Club Extra technology camp, he stood out again for how easily he learned, for his leadership and, above all, for his willingness to help other children. Thanks to a GuateStar scholarship, he finished 8th grade in 2026 and starts 9th grade in 2027. He has received awards for academic excellence, conduct and attitude.

His greatest achievement is what he has chosen to do with the opportunities he received: today he volunteers at Club Extra, sharing what he knows and helping other children in his community.

Jonathan’s path along the Pathway

  • 2020In 4th grade, he receives a computer and access to MAYALINK to study during the pandemic.
  • On his ownWorks ahead through the 5th- and 6th-grade videos and learns math without going to school.
  • Stage 1At Club Extra, he stands out at a technology camp for his willingness to help others.
  • ScholarshipAttends middle school on a GuateStar scholarship: in 2026 he finishes 8th grade.
  • Stage 2Volunteers at Club Extra and receives new honors.
  • 2027Starts 9th grade.
  • His dreamTo be a lawyer, support his family and serve others.

A computer can spark curiosity; a scholarship can keep a dream alive; and an opportunity can shape a young person able to multiply that impact in others.
