Iñaki and Aragón: two dreams, a love that never gives up

A story of faith, perseverance and opportunities that transform lives.

📍 TotonicapánThe Pathway: Scholarship → Stage 22026 annual report
  1. Home
  2. Stories
  3. Iñaki and Aragón

For Don Óscar and his wife, being parents has meant much more than watching their children grow. It has been a journey of love, sacrifice and constant effort so that Iñaki and Aragón, two brothers who needed a different kind of support, have the chance to learn, develop and build their own future.

In 2019, convinced that their sons deserved the same opportunities to grow and reach their dreams, they came to GuateStar seeking educational support. Both brothers received scholarships and began taking part in Club Extra.

The first years were full of challenges. GuateStar decided to adapt its support to their needs and assigned them a facilitator dedicated exclusively to working with them. With patience, personalized attention and their parents’ unwavering commitment, small steps forward began to turn into big achievements.

“Truly transformative education doesn’t mean requiring everyone to learn the same way, but offering each person the support they need to develop their abilities.”GuateStar

Two brothers, two dreams

Iñaki · wants to be a dentist

He finished 9th grade in 2025 and chose to study dentistry. In 2026 he completed his first year of a high school program focused on dentistry and is beginning to take part in dental outreach for low-income people.

Aragón · wants to be a chef

In 2026 he completed 8th grade. He dreams of being, as he enthusiastically puts it, “a pizza chef.” His path could continue with technical training at INTECAP.

Success doesn’t look just one way

Today both brothers also volunteer at Club Extra and in Mentors, sharing with others the opportunities they have received. Behind each of their achievements are the hands of their parents, who have never stopped standing by them, and the generosity of those who have believed in their potential. For the family, faith in God has been a source of strength throughout this journey.

Iñaki dreams of being a dentist. Aragón dreams of making pizzas. Two different paths, equally worthy, united by their parents’ love.
