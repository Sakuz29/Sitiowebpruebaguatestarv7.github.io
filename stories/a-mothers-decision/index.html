A mother's decision

“Try to study here in Guatemala first.” Four decisions that changed a whole family.

📍 Chuixacol, MomostenangoThe Pathway: Stage 5 → Stage 62026 annual report
  1. Home
  2. Stories
  3. A mother's decision

Ten years ago in Chuixacol, a village of just 18 families in Momostenango, one family’s life changed. The father emigrated to the United States, and by the second year the letters and the money stopped coming.

Some relatives had already spoken with a smuggler to take the son across the border. His plan was to emigrate. His mother asked him for just one thing: “Try to study here in Guatemala first.” Then she went to GuateStar to ask for an opportunity.

Four decisions

  1. A mother decided to fight for her son

    She asked him to study in Guatemala before emigrating and reached out to GuateStar. He received a full scholarship.

    About to emigrate → 100% scholarship

  2. He decided not to give up

    His home was 33 kilometers (20 miles) from school and he couldn’t afford the fare. Instead of saying “I can’t,” he asked “What can I do?” and found other passengers to ride with him in exchange for the trip.

    0 children in his village → 7 in the first Club Extra

  3. He decided to go further

    GuateStar supported him with a scholarship to study Agroforestry Education in Sololá. He spent two years away from his family during the pandemic, and then made it to college.

    Unfinished elementary school in his village → High school and college

  4. And he decided to come back

    Today Franzi runs his own farming business, works for an international organization and volunteers at GuateStar. He has even presented his project at Universidad del Valle.

    He received an opportunity → Today he creates them for others
“GuateStar didn’t just give me the opportunity to break out of poverty. It changed my life, and the lives of the generations that come after me.”Franzi

The next generation

Today his sister Rebeca studies on a GuateStar scholarship, at the same school where he studied. Each decision seemed small. Together, they changed the life of the whole family.

What would have happened to Franzi if no one had believed in him? His story is the story of tens of thousands in Guatemala. Many children already have the curiosity and the drive; what’s missing is someone to walk with them.
