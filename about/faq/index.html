About GuateStar

What is GuateStar?

A 501(c)(3) nonprofit based in the United States with an office in San Cristóbal Totonicapán. For decades it has supported children in the Guatemalan highlands through quality education, holistic support and leadership development.

Where do you work?

In eight communities in the department of Totonicapán, in the municipalities of San Bartolo Aguas Calientes, Totonicapán and San Andrés Xecul. See the list of communities.

Why Totonicapán?

Because it’s one of the departments with the lowest college enrollment in the country. In Guatemala, of every 100 children who start elementary school, only about 11 reach college. Informal work and migration pull many young people out of school.

Are you a religious organization?

GuateStar builds values (love, integrity, innovation, collaboration and hope), and its history is tied to faith, as told in the book IMPULSOS. It works with two Evangelical Methodist schools, and its programs serve children without discrimination based on race, sex, color, religion or ethnic origin.

About the programs

What is Club Extra?

Learning groups in children’s own communities, with educational and technology activities built on the CREA+ method, created by GuateStar. In 2026, 534 children enrolled. More about Club Extra.

How are scholarships awarded?

Through responsible and transparent selection processes, to support families with real needs and a strong commitment to their children’s education. We ask scholarship students to work hard on their grades, act responsibly and serve.

How do you measure results?

What matters most to us is every child moving on to the next level. We keep records by community and by student: weekly attendance, use of the Chispa platform, scholarship students advancing a grade and graduates going on to college. See our impact.

How do you protect children?

The wellbeing of every child comes before any communication or fundraising goal. We publish photos and stories only with the family’s consent, never share information that could locate a child, and our staff follow a code of conduct. Child protection policy.

About giving

Is my donation tax-deductible?

GuateStar is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit registered in the United States, so gifts from U.S. taxpayers are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law. If you give from another country, it depends on your local laws. You’ll receive a receipt by email. GuateStar does not provide tax advice.

Can I change or cancel my monthly gift?

Yes, at any time. Write to us through the contact form and the cancellation takes effect before the next charge.

Why do donations go through Colorado Gives?

Colorado Gives Foundation is the platform GuateStar uses to receive online donations from the United States. Payment is processed securely in US dollars, and you receive the receipt by email.

Can I give from Guatemala?

Yes, by bank transfer in quetzales to GuateStar’s account at Banco Industrial. If you need an invoice, leave your NIT in the transfer notice form.

How is my gift used?

Your gift supports the educational programs: scholarships, technology, mentorship and support for families. Gifts are allocated to the area of greatest need.

Can I donate computers?

Yes, if they’re in good condition. Write to us before sending them so we can coordinate. More information.

Volunteering and visits

Can I volunteer?

Yes. If you have a talent to share, from technology to design, languages or professional mentoring, write to us and let’s talk about how you can support the children, in person or remotely.

Can I visit the programs?

Write to us in advance. We coordinate every visit so it respects the children’s routine and our child protection policy.

Didn’t find your answer?

Write to us. There are people on the other side, not an automated inbox.
